## Interpretation

CC BY-NC permits noncommercial sharing/adaptation subject to attribution and other terms, but it does not automatically settle whether distributing trained model weights or generated font files is noncommercial or whether those outputs are adaptations of the source font. The exact license version and licensor's authority must be verified for each asset. No font listed above is approved for ML training at this stage.
