# Font creator permission request template

Use this message for each font creator or rights holder. Replace bracketed fields and attach the exact font filename(s) and SHA-256 hashes once acquired.

---

Subject: Permission request — non-commercial Arabic AI font research and training

Hello [Creator / Rights Holder],

I am an independent developer working on a free, non-commercial research project to build an AI model that learns Arabic letterforms and generates Arabic font designs.

I would like to ask for your written permission to use the following font files:

- Font / family: [name]
- Exact files: [filenames]
- Source URL: [URL]
- SHA-256: [hashes, once verified]

The intended use is:
1. Download and preserve the font files and their source/license notices.
2. Render glyphs and Arabic text samples from the fonts.
3. Use the font files and rendered samples as training and evaluation data for a machine-learning model.
4. Keep the resulting trained model and generated font files free and non-commercial.
5. Publish the model weights and generated font files for free non-commercial use, with attribution and any license conditions you specify.

No font files or generated outputs will be sold, and I do not plan to use the project for commercial advantage.

Could you please confirm in writing whether you authorize these uses, including:
- machine-learning training on the font outlines and rendered glyph images;
- distribution of the trained model weights for free non-commercial use;
- distribution of newly generated font files for free non-commercial use;
- whether attribution, source notices, or a particular license must accompany the dataset, model, or generated fonts;
- whether any parts of the font (for example, Latin glyphs or glyphs sourced from another project) have separate rights holders or restrictions.

If you can, please state the exact license version or permission terms that apply. If you are not the rights holder for every part of the font, please identify the relevant rights holders.

Thank you for your time and for creating Arabic typefaces.

Best regards,
[Your name]

---

## Project-side record

Do not treat silence, a download page, or a generic “free for personal use” label as permission. Store the reply, date, sender identity/authority, exact font filenames, hashes, scope of permission, and any conditions. A permission for personal design use does not automatically authorize ML training or redistribution of model weights.
